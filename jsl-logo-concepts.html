Five directions for one signature

Pulled straight from the portfolio's own language: Jost at 700 weight, the teal that runs through every page (#069494), and the soft gradient pools that sit behind the work — rendered here on both the light and the photography-dark ground, exactly as the site switches between them.

Teal · #069494 Teal Dark · #046868 Photography Ground · #0a1615 Seafoam · #e1f9f4
01Aperture Mark

Six blades of a lens iris, tuned to the same fall-off as the background blobs — the shutter closing around a blank centre reads as both "camera" and "focal point," the two things the portfolio is actually about.

02Wave Monogram · JL

J and L drawn as one continuous, curved stroke — the same soft, water-like line that drifts through the site's backgrounds, just pulled tight enough to read as initials at a glance.

03Primary Wordmark

Straight off the hero's own name treatment — bold, uppercase, tight tracking — with a single teal full stop standing in for "Lee." The most reusable mark: header, email signature, business card.

04Hex Frame

The hexagon is the site's own hairline motif, borrowed here as a badge for a single initial. Solid on light, outlined on dark — built to survive being shrunk to a 32px favicon.

05Editorial Signature

Borrows Newsreader italic — the same serif the site reserves for quotes and the media shelf — for a quieter, personal mark. Pairs well as a sign-off on the Photography and About pages specifically.

06Tilted Tile

A rounded square tipped 6°, echoing the tilt every project card on the homepage rests at before it straightens on hover. Reads cleanly as a tab icon or app tile at any size.

Recommendation: pair 03 · Primary Wordmark for full-width use (site header, resume, PDF exports) with 06 · Tilted Tile as the compact mark (favicon, social avatar) — between them they cover every size the current site already needs, without introducing a font or colour the portfolio doesn't already use.
